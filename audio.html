Experimental Field Recording Max/MSP 2026

Youth II

An experimental electronic composition built from sampled 1950s American educational films and original synthesizers designed in Reaktor, exploring nostalgia, optimism, and the uncanny through fragmented archival voices and evolving electronic textures.

Concept

This piece reimagines the optimistic language of mid-century American educational media through repetition, distortion, and synthesis. Familiar classroom narration is transformed into an abstract soundscape where cheerful certainty gradually gives way to ambiguity, questioning the ideals embedded within these archival recordings.

Tools

Native Instruments Reaktor • Ableton Live • Archival 1950s Educational Film Samples • Custom Synthesizers • Audio Editing & Sampling

Process

Original synthesizers were designed in Reaktor and combined with carefully selected samples from 1950s educational films. Through granular manipulation, filtering, time-stretching, and layering, spoken dialogue and instructional sounds were reconstructed into rhythmic and atmospheric elements, creating a composition that balances vintage Americana with contemporary electronic sound design.

SOUND DESIGN

Sound Design Ableton Live Foley 2026

Fantastika

A complete sound redesign for a sequence from Fantastika, reimagining the film through original foley, ambience, dialogue editing, and expressive sound effects. Created for a sound design course, the project explores how carefully crafted audio can reshape narrative, atmosphere, and emotional pacing.

Concept

This project reconstructs the film's sonic world from the ground up, replacing existing audio with an original soundtrack designed to heighten immersion and storytelling. Every sound—from subtle environmental ambience to character movement and dramatic effects—was considered as part of a cohesive auditory experience that supports the visual narrative.

Tools

Ableton Live • Foley Recording • Field Recording • Sound Libraries • Audio Editing • Mixing • Layering • Equalization • Reverb

Process

The project began with spotting the film to identify every sound required for the scene. Original foley recordings were captured and combined with carefully selected ambience and sound effects before being edited, layered, synchronized, and mixed inside Ableton Live. Attention was given to timing, spatial depth, and dynamic contrast to create a believable and expressive soundscape that complements the animation while establishing its own distinct atmosphere.
