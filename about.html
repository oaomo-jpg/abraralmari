About

Hi, I'm Abrar.

I'm a creative technologist, designer, and researcher interested in the ways technology shapes culture, memory, and storytelling. My work blends creative coding, immersive media, sound, interaction design, and anthropology to create experiences that invite curiosity and participation.

I recently completed degrees in Integrated Design & Media and Anthropology at New York University, where I explored how digital technologies can communicate history, identity, and collective memory through interactive installations and experimental media.

Whether I'm building an interactive website, programming generative visuals, producing audio, or conducting ethnographic research, I'm interested in creating work that is playful, thoughtful, and deeply human.

My current interests include creative coding, audiovisual performance, speculative design, archives, projection mapping, generative art, digital storytelling, and emerging technologies.
